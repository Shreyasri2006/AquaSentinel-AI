HOW TO READ THE MAP

Risk levels combine multiple signals

AquaSentinel separates information into two main signal groups before producing an overall prototype risk level.

B1

Environmental risk

Water quality, rainfall, temperature, sanitation and other environmental factors.

B2

Health surveillance

Community reports, symptom patterns and available disease-surveillance information.

AI

Risk fusion

Combines B1 and B2 indicators to present an explainable prototype risk category.

i

Prototype demonstration data

The values displayed on this map are for academic and prototype demonstration purposes. They are not official medical diagnoses or government-issued public-health warnings.
