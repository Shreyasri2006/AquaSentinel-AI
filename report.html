Reporting guidance

What makes a useful report?

  • Provide the clearest possible location.
  • Describe what you directly observed.
  • Mention when the problem began.
  • Avoid making an unverified diagnosis.
  • Protect the privacy of affected people.
P

Privacy first

Do not upload medical records, identity documents or photographs showing identifiable people.

V

Verification required

Community reports are preliminary signals. Professional field and laboratory verification is always required.
